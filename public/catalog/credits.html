Norva Selection — Credits

Films and live television from several public playlists, together in Norva Selection. Norva access is included during your eligible 7-day trial, then requires a Norva subscription.

Use the source's Sync action in Norva to refresh the selection. Films appear in Movies; television appears in Live TV. Repeated media URLs are merged. Regional editions, languages and alternate streams can still represent the same programme.

Media stays with its original hosts. Availability, advertising and territorial restrictions depend on each provider. Inclusion is not an endorsement or a promise that every stream works in every country. Norva does not remove advertising, credits or access restrictions.

Other sources researched

  • IPTV-org general playlist — Removed from Selection. The general playlist was retired after playback checks. The separate Movies playlist remains included as live cinema channels, not on-demand films.
  • Samsung TV Plus — Removed from Selection. Removed after playback checks. Samsung delivery URLs are also excluded from the aggregate playlists in Norva Selection.
  • m3u8-xtream-playlist — Unavailable. The movies and series endpoint does not resolve. Its public TV links are already covered by IPTV-org.
  • Movies Deluxe — Requires a connector. 26,700 records referencing Archive.org and YouTube pages, including clips and incorrect matches; these are not direct media URLs.
  • PublicDomainM3U series — Requires a series adapter. One episode. The films playlist is included above.
  • FastChannels — Generator. Self-hosted connectors, not an additional ready-to-play catalogue.
  • yt-movies-m3u — Requires a YouTube player. YouTube content needs its official player integration.
  • m3u8_creator — Generator. Playlist generation tool, not a separate content provider.
  • Free Official YouTube Content — Directory. Official international YouTube channels; not an M3U feed.
  • Internet Archive — Media host. Hosts the Blender films and PublicDomainM3U media already included.
  • Sita Sings the Blues — Individual film under review. The creator specifies music exceptions alongside the film licence.
  • VOD-Movies-Playlist-M3U — No usable VOD feed. The advertised VOD file has no entries; another file contains unidentified television streams.
