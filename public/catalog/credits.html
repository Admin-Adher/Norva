Other sources researched

  • IPTV-org general playlist — Removed from Selection. The general playlist was retired after playback checks. The separate Movies playlist is also withdrawn during the full review; it contains live cinema channels, not on-demand films.
  • Samsung TV Plus — Removed from Selection. Removed after playback checks. Samsung delivery URLs are also excluded from the aggregate playlists in Norva Selection.
  • m3u8-xtream-playlist — Unavailable. The movies and series endpoint does not resolve. Its public TV links are already covered by IPTV-org.
  • Movies Deluxe — Requires a connector. 26,700 records referencing Archive.org and YouTube pages, including clips and incorrect matches; these are not direct media URLs.
  • PublicDomainM3U series — Requires a series adapter. One episode in the earlier audit. Both films and series are withdrawn during the full review.
  • FastChannels — Generator. Self-hosted connectors, not an additional ready-to-play catalogue.
  • yt-movies-m3u — Requires a YouTube player. YouTube content needs its official player integration.
  • m3u8_creator — Generator. Playlist generation tool, not a separate content provider.
  • Free Official YouTube Content — Directory. Official international YouTube channels; not an M3U feed.
  • Internet Archive — Media host. Hosts the previously included Blender films and PublicDomainM3U media. These are withdrawn during the full review.
  • Sita Sings the Blues — Individual film under review. The creator specifies music exceptions alongside the film licence.
  • VOD-Movies-Playlist-M3U — No usable VOD feed. The advertised VOD file has no entries; another file contains unidentified television streams.
