1. Eligibility and verification

You must be an individual who is old enough and has the legal capacity to enter this agreement in your country. You must provide accurate information and complete the identity, age, country and capacity checks required for your jurisdiction. Norva may place an application or account on hold, request a new verification, or reject access where required by law, provider rules or a documented fraud signal. A company or person acting for a company cannot join this pilot.

2. Referral links and disclosure

Your link and QR code are personal, revocable and may not be sold or transferred. Whenever you share them, you must keep Norva's current disclosure next to the link so that a prospective customer understands that you may receive a commission. You must not hide the disclosure, promise guaranteed savings or earnings, impersonate Norva, use misleading advertising, spam, bid on protected Norva brand terms where prohibited, or promote unlawful media access.

Minimum plain-language disclosure: “Partner link · I may receive 20% of eligible Norva payments excluding tax. Earnings are not guaranteed. Norva is a media player; no content or TV subscription is included.”

The disclosure must be clear and conspicuous, in the same language and close to the link or recommendation, before a person acts on it. A label such as “affiliate link” by itself is not enough where the audience may not understand it. Video, live and audio promotions must also disclose the relationship in the promotion itself in a form the audience can perceive. You remain responsible for any additional advertising rule that applies where you publish.

3. Attribution

A click creates only a short-lived, opaque referral claim. Attribution occurs only after the referred person authenticates and the server validates the current attribution window, account eligibility, self-referral, duplicate and abuse rules. Pairing or scanning a TV QR code is a relay to a trusted phone/browser and is not, by itself, an attribution or payment event. Norva's server ledger is authoritative.

4. Commission

The pilot rate is 20% of eligible Norva subscription payments received from a valid attributed customer while that customer and the partner remain eligible under the active programme version. The base is the amount actually paid after discounts and excluding tax, before deducting Google Play, RevenueCat, Revolut or bank fees. “Eligible” excludes refunds, chargebacks, disputed or fraudulent transactions and any item the applicable programme schedule expressly excludes. No amount is payable when the amount paid, tax, currency/exponent, transaction lineage or another required authoritative fact is missing. A separately itemized discount is optional context and is never subtracted twice from a store total that already reflects it.

A commission starts as pending and ordinarily matures no earlier than 45 days after the underlying eligible payment. Refunds, chargebacks, reversals, duplicate provider events and corrections may create a counter-entry before or after maturation or payout. A negative balance may be offset against future valid commissions, subject to applicable law.

5. Payouts and tax

Payouts begin only after Norva enables them for your jurisdiction, the minimum threshold is reached, the maturation period has elapsed, verification remains valid, and the required individual tax and tokenized payout profile is complete. The available currency, provider, timing, fees and minimum threshold are shown before you accept the applicable programme version. You are responsible for declaring and paying taxes that apply to your income. Norva may withhold or report amounts where legally required.

For the initial supported corridors, individual payouts are executed through Revolut Business. Norva prepares an exact payout batch and unique transfer reference; an authorized Finance operator validates and enters the transfer in Revolut, and Norva settles the commission ledger only after separate reconciliation against completed statement evidence. This operational workflow does not change your displayed commission amount or the jurisdiction-specific fees disclosed before activation.

Norva may hold a commission or payout while investigating a concrete fraud, sanctions, account-takeover, duplicate-attribution or payment-dispute signal. Administrative actions must be audited and role-limited. Norva will not treat a missing provider configuration or an exhausted free KYC quota as proof of fraud. Where reasonably possible, you will be told the public reason and the next available step without exposing security controls.

Norva may change future programme terms, rates or availability with notice appropriate to the change and applicable law. A new version is not retroactively applied to an already recorded eligible payment. You may stop sharing your link and request closure. Norva may suspend or close participation for a material breach, legal requirement or documented abuse. Valid, matured amounts remain subject to the rules and legal obligations in force for those entries. If a payout, return or recovery is still open, account deletion may remain pending only for the time needed to complete that account-specific financial closure; Support will provide the available next step.
