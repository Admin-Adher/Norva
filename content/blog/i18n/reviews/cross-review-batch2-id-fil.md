## Substantive fidelity checks

- **028:** six fictional records, three numbered episodes, the unplaced special,
  alternative version and different same-name series remain distinct. Partial
  progress and next-episode order are not conflated. No real merge is claimed.
- **161:** the fictional queries retain the zero/two/one-result sequence and their
  stated matching assumptions. The reused English screenshot, Albanian facet,
  twelve-version result, `Clear all` and `Back` remain bounded by the recorded web
  observation; neither native-client testing nor playback availability is inferred.
- **422:** screen mirroring, sender/receiver casting and independent-device handoff
  remain separate. Conditional receiver support and current product limitations
  survive; the scenarios do not establish simultaneous-use rights.
- **442:** Alex and Sam, episode 3 versus episode 7, and differing language
  preferences remain illustrative. Profiles are not security boundaries, device
  counts, parental-control guarantees or guaranteed additional source rights.
- **502:** embedded, separate, image-based and burned-in subtitles retain their
  distinctions. The four hypothetical rows, track-support conditions and the
  `00:18` cue are preserved. Turning a track off does not prove its storage origin,
  and no arbitrary subtitle import or universal MKV compatibility is promised.
- **561:** the same `00:42–00:52` scene and `1920×1080` dimensions support only the
  stated picture observations. Two uninterrupted short replays do not certify a
  network or identify unavailable camera/encoder facts. Draft-reference status
  and compatibility limits are retained.
- **582:** `0.20 × 0.5 = 0.10` and `0.60 × 0.5 = 0.30` remain sample-peak arithmetic,
  not LUFS, perceived loudness or acoustic exposure. Timing landmarks, measurement
  limits, conditional settings and qualified safe-listening advice remain intact.
- **602:** the medians `82` and `28 Mbps`, busy-window range `14–31 Mbps`, delay
  `18/65 ms`, jitter `3/24 ms`, test durations and bit/byte relationship are intact.
  One-way delay, round-trip delay, tool-defined variation and packet loss remain
  distinct. There is no universal acceptable-jitter threshold or causal claim from
  the fictional co-occurrence of pauses and weaker test results.
- **662:** all four fictional launches preserve first-frame, usable-navigation and
  artwork times. The `4.4–4.6` versus `1.2–1.4` second comparison is correct; Home
  and a thirty-second wait do not certify a warm/hot process. Manual timing is not
  represented as instrumented TTID/TTFD or a Norva performance benchmark.
- **721:** the example retains `8.0→5.0`, `10.2→7.2` and `12.0→15.0 GB`, an
  unclassified `2.2 GB` balance, and a chosen `6.0 GB` reserve leaving `9.0 GB`.
  Download completion, offline playability and finished viewing remain separate.
  No universal reserve, automatic media-byte sync or destructive blanket reset is
  recommended. The player/no-included-content positioning is preserved.

Across the set, authorized-source and compatibility conditions remain explicit.
Fictional examples remain labeled as fictional, while actual source evidence keeps
its limitations. Original English UI labels and image bytes are not altered to
imply a localized product test. No new benchmark, inventory, rights grant or
product capability has been introduced by translation.
