## Reproducible arithmetic

- Pixels per frame: `1920 × 1080 = 2,073,600`.
- Ten minutes: `10 × 60 = 600 seconds`.
- Assumed 4 Mbps average video rate: `4 × 600 ÷ 8 = 300 decimal MB`.
- Assumed 8 Mbps average video rate: `8 × 600 ÷ 8 = 600 decimal MB`.
- The diagram's 528-unit bar is twice the 264-unit bar. Both schematic frame rectangles are 256 by 144, the same 16:9 aspect ratio. Grid lines are explicitly schematic, not a rendered 1920-by-1080 pixel grid.
- The rates are assumptions. Excluded: audio, subtitle, container, encryption and network overhead. No calculation infers a quality score or uses peak bitrate as an average.
